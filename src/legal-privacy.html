1. Who we are

This website is operated by Paradise Kafé ("Paradise Kafé," "we," "us"), a café located at 552 N New Street, Bethlehem, Pennsylvania 18018. This policy explains how information is handled when you visit this website. It does not cover in-store purchases, which are handled by our point-of-sale provider under its own terms, or our social media pages, which are governed by those platforms' policies.

2. What we collect

Information you give us

None. The site has no accounts, contact forms, newsletter sign-ups, online ordering or payment fields. If you message us on social media or speak with us in the café, that communication is handled outside this website.

Information collected automatically

Like every website, when your browser requests a page our hosting provider (Vercel Inc.) automatically processes standard technical data so the page can be delivered and the service kept secure. This may include your IP address, browser type and version, device type, the page requested, the referring page, and the date and time of the request. We do not use this information to identify you or build a profile about you.

Features such as the "Open now" indicator, the menu search and the season switcher run entirely inside your browser. Nothing you type into the menu search is sent anywhere or saved.

3. How information is used

  • To deliver the website to your device and keep it running reliably.
  • To detect, prevent and respond to abuse, attacks and security incidents.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling or automated decision-making.

5. Cookies & tracking

This website does not set any cookies and does not use local storage, tracking pixels, fingerprinting or analytics. Because nothing non-essential is placed on your device, there is no cookie banner. If you choose to load the Google map, Google may set its own cookies; you can avoid this by using the "Get directions" links instead, which open in a new tab.

We honour Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals by default, since we don't track or sell data in the first place.

6. Sharing & selling

We do not sell, rent or "share" (as that term is used in U.S. state privacy laws for cross-context behavioural advertising) any personal information. Data may be disclosed only to the service providers listed above to run the site, or where required by law, such as a valid court order.

7. Retention

We don't keep a database of visitors. Hosting request logs are retained by Vercel for a limited period according to its own retention schedule and then deleted.

8. Security

The site is served only over encrypted HTTPS with HTTP Strict Transport Security, a strict Content Security Policy that blocks unauthorised scripts, protection against clickjacking and content-type sniffing, and a restrictive permissions policy that denies access to your camera, microphone, location and other device features. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, but we keep the site as simple as possible to minimise risk.

9. Children's privacy

This website is for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will address it.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to know what personal information is held about you, to request deletion or correction, and to opt out of sale or targeted advertising. Because this site does not collect personal information directly, we generally hold nothing to provide, correct or delete, but you are welcome to contact us with any request and we will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

11. Changes to this policy

If we add features that change how data is handled (for example, online ordering or a newsletter), we will update this policy and the "Last updated" date above before those features go live.
