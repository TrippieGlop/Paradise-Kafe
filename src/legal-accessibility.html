Paradise Kafé wants everyone to be able to explore our menu and plan a visit. We aim for this website to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA.

What we've done

  • A "Skip to content" link and a logical heading structure on every page.
  • Full keyboard navigation with visible focus indicators, including the menu filters, season tabs and crêpe/croissant switch.
  • Menu prices exposed as text, with each item labelled for screen readers including both cup sizes.
  • Colour contrast checked for body text against both dark and cream backgrounds.
  • Animations are decorative and switch off automatically when your device has "reduce motion" turned on.
  • Responsive layouts that work from small phones to large screens and support browser zoom up to 400%.
  • Decorative illustrations are hidden from assistive technology so they don't add noise.

Known limitations

The optional Google map is a third-party embed and may not be fully accessible; the address and "Get directions" links are always available as text alternatives.

Feedback

If something on the site doesn't work for you, please tell us. Message us on Instagram or let us know in the café at 552 N New Street, Bethlehem, PA. We're also happy to read the menu to you or answer questions in person.
